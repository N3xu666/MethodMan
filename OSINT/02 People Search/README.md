# 02 People Search

People search is the most sensitive OSINT subcategory. It resolves an
identity from partial information (a name, an email, a username, a photo)
and connects it to public records, social accounts, and professional
history. Done carelessly, it produces attribution errors and privacy
harm. Done well, it is auditable and proportionate to the requirement.

## Scope

- Identity resolution: name, email, username, phone, photo.
- Social media platforms and their search surfaces.
- Public records: corporate registries, court filings, property records.
- Professional history: LinkedIn, GitHub, conference talks.
- Verification: disambiguation, cross-platform corroboration.
