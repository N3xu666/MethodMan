# Compliance and Regulation

Compliance is the state of meeting external obligations: laws,
regulations, industry standards and contractual requirements.
Regulation is the source of those obligations. This subcategory
covers the frameworks, the mapping process, the audit cycle and the
operational practices that keep an organization compliant without
turning compliance into theatre.

## Certification Alignment

This subcategory maps to the following certifications:

- **CISSP** - Domain 1 (Security and Risk Management): legal and
  regulatory issues, compliance requirements.
- **CISM** - Domain 1 (Information Security Governance): compliance
  with external requirements.
- **CISA** - Domain 1 (Information System Auditing Process): audit
  standards and compliance.
- **ISO 27001 LI/LA** - Clauses 4-10, Annex A.5.31 (Legal,
  statutory, regulatory and contractual requirements).
